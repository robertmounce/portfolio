About

I earned my B.A. in Economics from California State University, Long Beach. While at community college, before transferring, I taught myself Python, R, SQL, and relational databases through Coursera and personal projects, including data cleaning and data-driven decision making.

Before this I was Head Mechanic across 4 locations, responsible for 300+ bicycles and watercraft. Listening to Freakonomics and Planet Money showed me how much data could improve my own work, so I built dashboards for weekly meetings with the corporate mechanic that communicated far more information than before.

I'm interested in applying this skill set to economic and trade policy.
